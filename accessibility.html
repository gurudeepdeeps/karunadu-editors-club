Accessibility Statement

♿ Conformance Objective: WCAG 2.1 Guidelines • Design Review

Our Commitment: Karunadu Editors Club is committed to ensuring our community platform and resource directory are accessible to all digital creators, regardless of technology or ability.

1. Implemented Accessibility Features

Our website incorporates the following accessibility practices:

  • Keyboard Navigation: All navigation links and interactive controls are focusable via keyboard Tab key, and search can be opened with ⌘K / Ctrl+K and closed with Esc.
  • Semantic Structure: Pages are structured using appropriate HTML5 semantic landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <aside>, <article>, and <footer>).
  • Screen Reader Support: Informative ARIA roles (role="dialog", role="alert", role="listbox") and accessible labels are supplied for interactive overlays and icon buttons.
  • High Contrast Colors: Dark theme palette maintains strong contrast ratios between text elements and backgrounds for enhanced readability.
  • Responsive Typography: Clean modern typography using Inter font scale adapts across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

2. Ongoing Improvements

While we strive for broad accessibility, external download links to third-party services (such as Google Drive) operate under their respective interface constraints. We continuously review our HTML and CSS to improve standards compliance.

3. Accessibility Feedback & Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have difficulty accessing any section of our website, please let us know through our Discord #help-desk channel or submit an issue on GitHub. We appreciate your feedback and strive to make prompt corrections.
