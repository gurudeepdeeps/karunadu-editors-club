General Questions

Common questions about the community and resources.

1. Which software should I start with as a beginner?

👉 DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro are top choices — it depends on what style you want to learn.

If you're into cinematic color grading or film-style edits, go with DaVinci Resolve (free & professional).

If you're learning YouTube, social media, or commercial editing, Adobe Premiere Pro is widely used in the industry.

Both are powerful — your choice depends on the type of edits you want to master.

2. What specs do I need for editing?

👉 8–16GB RAM, SSD, and Intel processors are better for most editors.

For smooth performance:

  • RAM: 8–16GB minimum
  • Processor: Intel i5/i7 (preferred for stability in editing software)
  • Graphics Card: Dedicated GPU (NVIDIA or AMD)
  • Storage: SSD for faster loading and exports

3. Do I need a high-end PC to start editing?

👉 No, a decent laptop or desktop with the right specs is enough.

You don’t need a top-end machine. With mid-range specs (Intel i5, 8–16GB RAM, SSD), you can easily handle most editing tasks.

4. How do I improve my editing skills fast?

👉 Practice daily, follow pro editors, and recreate real edits.

  • Watch YouTube tutorials
  • Recreate edits from trending videos or movies
  • Participate in the Community Chat and editing forums
  • Build your own portfolio
  • Learn software shortcuts and editing workflows

5. Can I earn money from video editing?

👉 Yes, editing is a valuable skill that’s in high demand.

Ways to earn:

  • Freelance on Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer
  • Edit content for YouTubers, influencers, or businesses
  • Work with event companies, wedding studios, or marketing agencies
  • Sell your own templates, transitions, or LUTs

6. How much money can I make from editing?

👉 Beginners can make ₹10K/month, pros earn ₹1–2L+.

Your earnings depend on skill, speed, and niche. Even with a small client base, consistent work can lead to big income growth.

7. Do I need a degree to become an editor?

👉 No, your skills and portfolio matter more than a degree.

No formal education is required. Clients and studios care more about the quality of your work and your ability to deliver on time.

8. Where can I find free clips to practice editing?

👉 Try royalty-free stock sites like Pexels and Mixkit.

  • Pexels
  • Mixkit
  • Videvo

9. What’s the best format to export edited videos?

👉 Use MP4 (H.264) for online and general delivery.

  • MP4 (H.264) – Best for YouTube, Instagram, and web sharing
  • MOV/ProRes – Best for high-quality delivery or pro-level work

10. Where can I get free music and sound effects?

👉 Check out YouTube Audio Library, Mixkit, and Pixabay.

  • YouTube Audio Library
  • Mixkit Sounds
  • Pixabay Music
