Cookie Policy

🍪 Verification Status: Zero Trackers Deployed • Scope: karunadu-editors-club

Clear Disclosure: This website does not set tracking cookies, marketing cookies, third-party analytics cookies, or behavioral advertising pixels.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are typically used to remember user accounts, store basket contents, or monitor web traffic.

2. How We Use Browser Storage

Our code inspection of this website confirms:

  • No First-Party Tracking Cookies: We do not issue HTTP cookies or set persistent identifiers in your browser.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Pixels: We do not integrate tracking scripts (such as Facebook Pixel, Google AdSense, or affiliate trackers).
  • In-Memory Client State: The search shortcut (⌘K) and navigation interactions operate entirely in temporary browser memory during your active page session.

3. External Third-Party Services

When you click on links pointing to external third-party domains (such as Google Drive), those external sites may use their own cookies. Karunadu Editors Club has no control over cookies placed by external websites.

4. How You Can Control Cookies

Because we do not set tracking cookies, there is no cookie consent banner required to opt out. However, you can control or delete cookies from your browser settings at any time (e.g., through Chrome Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data).
