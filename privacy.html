Privacy Policy

📅 Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Scope: Public Static Website

Summary: Karunadu Editors Club is a free, static community catalog. We do not require user account registration, do not run user databases, do not collect personal payment details, and do not place tracking or advertising cookies on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike commercial platforms, our website operates without user accounts or server-side databases:

  • No User Accounts: We do not ask for, capture, or store your name, email address, password, or profile information.
  • No Payment Data: All resources and links are provided for free; we do not process debit/credit card numbers or billing details.
  • No Direct Tracking Cookies: The code written for this website sets zero analytics, advertising, or persistent profiling cookies in your browser.

2. Technical Log Data & Web Hosting

This static website is deployed via hosting providers (such as Vercel). When you request web pages, web servers routinely log standard internet protocol requests (such as your public IP address, browser user-agent header, and referring URL) solely for operational delivery, security protection against automated abuse, and DDoS defense.

We do not aggregate these logs with personal identities or sell any traffic data.

3. External Links & Third-Party Services

Our website contains external hyperlinks pointing to third-party services, including:

  • Google Drive: For hosting shared community files and creative assets.
  • Community Chat: For peer discussions, assistance, and creative collaboration.
  • GitHub: For project source code and issue reporting.

Once you click an external link, you leave our website. Third-party services are governed by their own independent privacy policies and terms of service. We advise checking their respective policies when using their services.

4. Children's Privacy

Our site provides editing resources and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any minor has provided identifiable data through community channels, please contact our community moderators immediately.

5. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

For questions or requests regarding our website's technical privacy practices, please contact us through our Community Chat room or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
